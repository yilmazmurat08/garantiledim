# Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

This policy explains how the Garantiledim Android app (the "App") handles your information. The App is developed by Lusnika. You can send your questions to yilmazmurat08@gmail.com.

## Summary

- The App does not ask you to create an account.
- The product details, receipts, invoices and photos you enter are never sent to us or to any other server. The App has no server of its own.
- All your information is stored only on your own phone.
- No advertising, analytics or tracking tools are used; your data is not shared with or sold to anyone.
- The internet connection is used only by Google Play Billing for the Premium purchase.

## Information you enter

While using the App you enter the following information yourself:

- Product details: product name, store, category, purchase and delivery dates, return and warranty end dates.
- Receipts and invoices: the photo or PDF files you add and the small previews created from them.
- Product photos.
- Profile details: the name shown on the home screen and an optional profile photo.
- Settings: reminder start, notification time and app language.

This information is saved only in the App's private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot access this storage, and neither can we.

## Permissions and why they are used

- Notifications (Android 13 and later): To remind you before return and warranty periods end. If you don't grant it, the App keeps working; you just won't receive reminders.
- Storage (Android 9 and earlier only): To save an invoice to the Downloads folder with "Download".
- Background work and running after restart: So that the daily deadline check continues after the phone restarts.
- Internet and Google Play purchases (Billing): So that you can buy Garantiledim Premium through Google Play and the purchase can be verified. These permissions are used only by the Google Play Billing library; the App does not send your information to the internet.

The App does not request camera, location, contacts or microphone permissions. With "Take a photo", the picture is taken by your phone's own camera app and only the photo you take is passed to the App. When you choose from the gallery or files, only the file you select is copied.

## Sharing and exporting

When you save an invoice with "Download", a copy of the file is written to your phone's shared Downloads folder. Other apps on your phone can access that copy; managing it is up to you. When you open an invoice in another app, the file is shown only to the app you choose.

## Premium purchase

When you buy Garantiledim Premium, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your card or payment details. To decide whether Premium should be unlocked, the App receives only the purchase status from Google Play (which product was bought and the purchase identifier) and stores it on your phone. Technical information about the purchase and about how the Google Play Billing library works is processed by Google and is subject to Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Backup file (Premium)

Profile & Settings > Back up writes your products, receipts and invoices, product photos, name and reminder settings into a single .zip file. The file is saved only where you choose (your phone, Google Drive or another app); we cannot access it. The file is not encrypted, so be careful who you share it with. Restoring from a backup replaces the products on the phone with those in the backup.

## Backup

If Google backup is turned on for your phone, Android may back up your product list and settings to your Google account through its own backup service. Receipt, invoice and photo files are not included in this cloud backup (this prevents Android's per-app backup limit from being exceeded, which would stop the backup altogether); in a direct phone-to-phone transfer, by cable or wirelessly, these files are moved as well. This backup and transfer are carried out by Android and Google; we have no access to them. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

## Google Play

When you download the App from Google Play, Google may process information as part of its own services (for example downloads, updates and, depending on your device settings, crash and performance reports). This processing is subject to Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Data retention and deletion

Your information stays on your phone until you delete it.

- To delete a product together with its receipt, invoice and photos, use "Delete product" on the product's edit screen.
- To delete all data, uninstall the App or use "Clear data" under your phone's Settings > Apps > Garantiledim > Storage.

Copies you saved to the Downloads folder are not deleted when the App is uninstalled; you can delete them separately.

## Your rights

Because the information you enter never leaves your phone and is never sent to us, we do not process it. For any questions about your rights under applicable data protection laws (including the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 and the EU GDPR), write to yilmazmurat08@gmail.com. If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you.

## Children

The App is not directed at children and does not collect personal data from anyone.

## Changes

We may update this policy. For example, if new features are added, how they handle information will be described here clearly and the change will be announced in the App. The current version is always available in the App under Profile & Settings > Privacy Policy.
